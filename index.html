Is a doula right for you?

Signs that tell you that you need a doula in your birth team

01

You feel anxious about labour

Fear of the unknown is normal. A doula helps you understand every stage so you can meet it with calm instead of worry.

02

You want a positive birth experience

You have hopes for how your birth feels — and you want someone in your corner helping you work towards them.

03

Your partner wants to help, but isn't sure how

I guide your husband or partner with simple, practical ways to support you, so you both feel confident on the day.

04

You want to make informed choices

Understand your options, ask the right questions and feel heard by your care team.

05

You had a difficult previous birth

Planning for a different experience this time? Continuous support can make all the difference.

06

You want continuous support

Midwives and doctors change shifts. Your doula stays with you — before, during and after birth.

What does a doula do?

A doula provides continuous emotional, physical and informational support before, during and after birth. A doula does not provide medical care — I work alongside your doctor or midwife.

Is a doula only for natural births?

No. I support all kinds of births — at home or in hospital, with or without pain relief, inductions and caesarean births.

Will my husband still have a role?

Yes! I support couples together. I show your partner practical ways to help and make sure they feel confident and included.

When should I book?

Ideally in your second trimester, so we have time to prepare together — but get in touch at any stage to check availability.

Do you offer online classes or consultations?

Consultations can be online or in person. Ask me about current class formats and dates.
